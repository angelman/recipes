Thai Basil Chicken (Pad Kra Pao) — Meal Prep Cooked ✓

Servings: 6

Difficulty: Medium

Per serving: ~500–600 cal, ~35–40g protein per serving (with rice)

From: Oct 12, 2026 (adapted from Hot Thai Kitchen's pad kra pao, scaled ×3)

Adapted from Hot Thai Kitchen's pad kra pao

Source: hot-thai-kitchen.com

meal-prepchickenthaispicy

The real-deal version: chilies and garlic pounded into a paste, chicken seared hard before the sauce goes in. Salty, spicy, fragrant. Cooked Oct 12, 2026.

Ingredients

  • 2 lb ground chicken (see note)
  • 12–18 Thai bird's eye chilies (12 = medium heat across the batch)
  • 15 cloves garlic
  • 3 bell peppers, chopped into bite-size pieces
  • 1 1/2 small yellow onions, diced
  • 4 1/2 cups Thai basil leaves, loosely packed
  • Neutral oil: a thin film for searing (skip if your skillet is nonstick) + 1 tbsp for the chili-garlic paste
  • Salt, a pinch
  • Sauce (mix in a bowl first): 3 tbsp oyster-style sauce
  • Sauce: 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • Sauce: 2 tbsp fish sauce
  • Sauce: 1 1/2 tbsp tamari (in place of dark/black soy sauce)
  • Sauce: 1 1/2 tbsp sugar
  • Sauce: 6 tbsp water
  • To serve: 3 cups uncooked brown jasmine rice (rice cooker, brown rice setting, extra time)

Steps

  1. Start the rice first — brown jasmine takes a while. Use your cooker's brown rice setting.
  2. Pound the chilies into a fine paste with a mortar and pestle. Add the garlic and pound into a rough paste. (No mortar? Mince both as finely as you can — the paste is what makes it fragrant.)
  3. Stir the sauce ingredients together in a small bowl until the sugar dissolves. Pick the basil leaves and set them by the stove.
  4. Heat your largest skillet over HIGH heat with a thin film of oil (skip the oil if your skillet is nonstick). Add the chicken and spread it out. Sear without moving until the underside is browned. Toss and keep cooking until the liquid evaporates and it starts to sizzle and brown again — this browning is where the flavor comes from. Season with a pinch of salt.
  5. Push the chicken to one side. Add 1 tbsp oil and the chili-garlic paste. Sauté until the garlic starts to turn golden, about 30 seconds.
  6. Toss everything together. Pour in the sauce and toss until the chicken is cooked through and coated, 1–2 minutes.
  7. Add the bell peppers and onion. Toss 1–2 minutes until crisp-tender.
  8. Turn OFF the heat. Add all the basil and toss just until wilted, about 20 seconds. Taste — more fish sauce for salt, a pinch of sugar for balance.
  9. Portion over rice into containers. Refrigerate up to 4 days.

Notes

  • Reheating: sprinkle a teaspoon or two of water over the top before microwaving to loosen the sauce.
  • You ordered 3 lb of chicken — this recipe uses 2 lb (Pailin's ratio). Use all 3 if you want it meatier (~45g protein/serving), or freeze the third pound.
  • No wok needed; a large skillet on full heat works. Don't crowd it — if your skillet is small, sear the chicken in two batches.
  • The fried egg is traditional but doesn't reheat well — skip it on prep portions, fry fresh if you want one on serving day.
  • Jarred garlic works in the paste, but fresh is better here since it's a main flavor — your call.
  • Cook corrections (Oct 12 cook): searing in stainless steel — use a thin film of oil, heated until shimmering (supersedes the earlier no-oil dry-sear guidance). Tamari stands in for dark/black soy sauce (Sprouts doesn't carry it). Jarred garlic was used. Actual cook used regular basil — Sprouts substituted it for the Thai basil in the pickup order.
