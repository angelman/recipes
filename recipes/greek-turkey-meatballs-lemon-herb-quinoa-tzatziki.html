Greek Turkey Meatballs with Lemon-Herb Quinoa & Tzatziki

Servings: ~5 lunch portions

Per serving: ~540 kcal, 48g protein, 7g fiber per serving

From: Oct 4, 2026

meal-prepturkeymediterranean

The Mediterranean one. Suggested in the Oct 4 round; set aside when turkey and Greek yogurt were ruled out that week.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lb ground turkey
  • 1 large egg
  • ½ cup panko breadcrumbs
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced (meatballs) + extra for tzatziki
  • 2 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 lemon (zest for meatballs, juice for quinoa and tzatziki)
  • Salt and pepper
  • 1.5 cups dry quinoa
  • Fresh parsley, chopped
  • 1 English cucumber
  • 1 pint cherry tomatoes
  • 1 red onion
  • 1.5 cups plain Greek yogurt

Steps

  1. Heat oven to 400°F. Mix ground turkey with egg, ½ cup panko, 3 minced garlic cloves, 2 tsp oregano, zest of 1 lemon, salt and pepper. Roll into 20 meatballs.
  2. Bake on a lightly sprayed sheet pan 18–20 min until 165°F inside.
  3. Simmer 1.5 cups rinsed quinoa in 3 cups salted water 15 min; fluff with juice of ½ lemon and chopped parsley.
  4. Tzatziki: grate cucumber, squeeze dry, stir into 1.5 cups Greek yogurt with minced garlic, lemon juice, and salt.
  5. Pack: quinoa base, 4 meatballs, cucumber-tomato-onion salad, tzatziki in a side cup.

Notes

  • Reheats beautifully and scales to a Tuesday dinner — extra meatballs over quinoa for two.
