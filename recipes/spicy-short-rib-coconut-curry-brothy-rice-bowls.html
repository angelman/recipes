Spicy Short Rib Coconut Curry Brothy Rice Bowls

Servings: See notes

From: Sept 28, 2026

By Brita (britacooks) — https://britacooks.com/short-rib-coconut-curry-brothy-rice-bowls/

beefpressure-cookercurry

Braised short rib in a coconut curry broth over rice. Extracted from Brita Cooks' recipe page.

Ingredients

  • 2–2.5 lbs bone-in short ribs
  • Kosher salt
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • 3 tbsp red curry paste
  • 2 shallots, halved
  • 4–5 cloves garlic, smashed
  • 4" piece ginger, sliced into coins (no need to peel)
  • 2 tsp soy sauce or tamari
  • 1 tsp fish sauce
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 3–4 cups beef stock
  • 1 can (13.5 oz) full-fat coconut milk
  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • Garnish: Thai basil, sliced green onions, sesame seeds, drizzle of chili oil (optional)

Steps

  1. Trim excess fat from the short ribs and season generously with kosher salt on all sides. Set the pressure cooker to sauté, add the oil, and sear the ribs deeply on all sides until well browned. Transfer to a plate.
  2. Drain most of the rendered fat, leaving about a tablespoon. Add the red curry paste and cook 2–3 minutes, stirring frequently and scraping up the browned bits, until slightly darkened and fragrant.
  3. Stir in the shallots, garlic, and ginger. Return the short ribs to the pot, add soy sauce, fish sauce, and brown sugar, then pour in enough beef stock to mostly submerge the ribs (3–4 cups).
  4. Seal the lid and pressure cook on high for 45 minutes.
  5. Natural release for 15 minutes, then quick release any remaining pressure.
  6. Transfer the ribs to a plate; once cool enough to handle, shred the meat off the bones.
  7. Strain the broth, discarding the shallots, garlic, and ginger. Return the broth to the pot, stir in the coconut milk, and adjust with a little more soy sauce or fish sauce to taste.
  8. Divide rice between bowls, top with shredded short rib, ladle the broth over, and finish with green onions, Thai basil, and sesame seeds.

Notes

  • Alternate methods: Dutch oven — braise covered at 325°F for ~3 hours. Slow cooker — low 8 hours or high 4–5 hours (sear and bloom the curry paste in a skillet first).
  • Full-fat canned coconut milk makes the creamiest broth; jasmine rice recommended.
  • Keeps 3–4 days in the fridge (store ribs, broth, and rice separately) or 3 months in the freezer.
