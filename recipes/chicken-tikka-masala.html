Chicken Tikka Masala

Servings: 4

From: Oct 17, 2026

From ChatGPT

Yogurt-marinated chicken in a creamy spiced tomato sauce. Meal-prep friendly.

Ingredients

  • Marinade: 1½ lb boneless, skinless chicken breast
  • Marinade: ½ cup fat-free plain Greek yogurt
  • Marinade: 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • Marinade: 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Marinade: 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • Marinade: 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated or minced
  • Marinade: 1 tsp garam masala
  • Marinade: 1 tsp ground cumin
  • Marinade: 1 tsp paprika or Kashmiri chili powder
  • Marinade: ½ tsp salt
  • Marinade: ¼ tsp black pepper
  • Sauce: 1 tbsp butter
  • Sauce: 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Sauce: 1 medium yellow onion, finely diced
  • Sauce: 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • Sauce: 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated or minced
  • Sauce: 1½ tsp garam masala
  • Sauce: 1 tsp ground cumin
  • Sauce: 1 tsp ground coriander
  • Sauce: 1 tsp paprika or Kashmiri chili powder
  • Sauce: ½ tsp turmeric
  • Sauce: ¼–½ tsp cayenne pepper, optional
  • Sauce: 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • Sauce: ½ cup chicken broth or water
  • Sauce: ½ cup heavy cream
  • Sauce: 1 tsp salt, plus more to taste
  • Sauce: 1 tsp sugar, optional
  • Sauce: ¼ cup chopped fresh cilantro or parsley
  • To serve: Brown or white rice

Steps

  1. Cut the chicken into roughly 1½-inch pieces. Combine the Greek yogurt, lemon juice, olive oil, garlic, ginger, garam masala, cumin, paprika or Kashmiri chili powder, salt, and black pepper in a bowl. Add the chicken and mix until thoroughly coated. Cover and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes (4–24 hours for best flavor).
  2. Heat a large skillet over medium-high to high heat with a little oil. Remove the chicken from the marinade, letting excess drip off. Sear in batches without overcrowding, 2–3 minutes per side, until browned (it doesn't need to be fully cooked). Transfer to a plate.
  3. Reduce heat to medium. Add the butter and olive oil, then the diced onion; cook 5–7 minutes until softened and lightly browned. Add the garlic and ginger and cook about 1 minute. Add the garam masala, cumin, coriander, paprika or Kashmiri chili powder, turmeric, and cayenne (if using); cook 30–45 seconds, stirring constantly.
  4. Add the crushed tomatoes and chicken broth. Scrape up any browned bits, add 1 tsp salt, and simmer gently uncovered for 10–15 minutes until slightly reduced and richer. If the tomatoes taste too acidic, add 1 tsp sugar.
  5. Return the chicken and any accumulated juices to the skillet. Simmer gently 5–8 minutes until the chicken is cooked through and reaches 165°F. Reduce heat to low, stir in the heavy cream, and simmer gently 2–3 more minutes — don't aggressively boil after adding the cream. Taste and adjust salt, cayenne, garam masala, or a small squeeze of lemon juice.
  6. Turn off the heat and stir in the chopped cilantro or parsley. Serve over brown or white rice.

Notes

  • Meal prep: divide into 4 containers with about ¼ of the tikka masala and 1¼–1½ cups cooked rice each. Keep a generous amount of sauce per portion — the rice absorbs some during storage. Let cool briefly, then refrigerate promptly.
  • Keeps 3–4 days in the refrigerator.
  • Reheating: microwave loosely covered in 60–90 second intervals, stirring the tikka masala between intervals if needed.
