Garlic-Lime Salmon with Sesame Edamame Barley Bowls

Servings: 4–6 portions

Per serving: ~38g protein, 10g fiber, ~580 kcal per serving

From: Sept 26, 2026

meal-prepsalmonhigh-protein

Seared salmon over barley with edamame and a lime-soy dressing. Suggested in the first meal-prep round; not picked.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lb salmon fillets
  • 1.5 cups pearl barley
  • 2 cups frozen edamame
  • 4 limes
  • Garlic
  • Low-sodium soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 4 scallions
  • 1 cucumber

Steps

  1. Simmer barley ~35 min until chewy.
  2. Sear salmon skin-side down 5–6 min, flip 2 min.
  3. Toss cooked barley with edamame, diced cucumber, scallions, and a dressing of lime juice, soy sauce, grated garlic, and sesame oil. Top with salmon and sesame seeds.

Notes

  • Storage: salmon holds 3 days refrigerated — eat this one Mon–Wed, freeze nothing.
