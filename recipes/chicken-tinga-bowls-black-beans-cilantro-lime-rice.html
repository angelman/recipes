Chicken Tinga Bowls with Black Beans & Cilantro-Lime Brown Rice

Servings: ~5 portions

Per serving: ~600 kcal, 48g protein, 13g fiber per serving

From: Oct 4, 2026

meal-prepchicken

The smoky one. Chicken thighs braised in chipotle-tomato sauce, spooned over rice with black beans.

Ingredients

  • 2 lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs
  • 7 oz chipotles in adobo sauce
  • 28 oz fire-roasted diced tomatoes
  • 2 cans (15 oz) black beans, drained
  • 1 bunch fresh cilantro
  • 4 limes
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1.5 cups brown rice
  • Salt to taste

Steps

  1. Sear 2 lb thighs in a hot pot 3 min per side; set aside.
  2. In the same pot, sauté diced onion 4 min, add 3 minced garlic cloves, 2 chopped chipotles + 1 tbsp adobo sauce; 1 min.
  3. Pour in the tomatoes plus 1 cup water; nestle thighs back in, cover, simmer 25 min until shreddable. Shred with two forks.
  4. Stir in drained black beans; simmer 5 more min. Salt to taste.
  5. Cook 1.5 cups brown rice; toss with chopped cilantro and juice of 2 limes. Pack: rice, tinga, extra lime wedges.

Notes

  • Scales up nicely for Tuesday dinner with your girlfriend — a crowd-pleaser.
