Chipotle Turkey & Black Bean Burrito Bowls Cooked ✓

Servings: 5 portions

Per serving: ~580 cal, ~46g protein, ~13g fiber per serving

From: Sept 26, 2026

meal-prepturkeyhigh-proteinhigh-fiber

Meal-prep burrito bowls with a chipotle kick. The first cook in the meal-prep series.

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb ground turkey
  • 2 (15-oz) cans black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1½ cups dry brown rice
  • 1 bag (16 oz) frozen corn
  • 2 bell peppers, diced
  • 1 red onion, diced
  • 2–3 chipotle peppers in adobo, minced, plus 1 tbsp of the sauce (7-oz can)
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tsp cumin, 2 tsp smoked paprika, salt
  • 2 limes, juiced
  • Plain Greek yogurt, for topping
  • 1 tbsp oil

Steps

  1. Cook the brown rice per package; fluff and squeeze half a lime over it.
  2. Heat oil in a large skillet over medium-high. Sauté onion 3–4 min until softened, add garlic, cumin, paprika, and chipotles; stir 30 seconds until fragrant.
  3. Add ground turkey, break it up, and cook until browned and cooked through, about 8 min.
  4. Stir in black beans and corn; cook 4–5 min until heated through. Season with salt and remaining lime juice.
  5. In a separate hot pan, quickly char the bell peppers 2–3 min so they stay crisp — no mush.
  6. Portion into containers: rice, turkey-bean mix, peppers. Pack the Greek yogurt separately and dollop on when eating.

Notes

  • Tip: slightly undercook the rice if you plan to microwave the bowls — it'll finish in the reheat without going soft.
