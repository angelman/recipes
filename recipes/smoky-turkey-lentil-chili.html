Smoky Turkey & Lentil Chili

Servings: 4–6 portions

Per serving: ~32g protein, 11g fiber, ~480 kcal per serving

From: Sept 26, 2026

meal-prepturkeyfreezer-friendlyhigh-fiber

One-pot chili with red lentils. Suggested in the first meal-prep round; not picked.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lb ground turkey
  • 1.5 cups red lentils
  • 1 large can diced tomatoes
  • 1 onion
  • 2 bell peppers
  • Garlic
  • Smoked paprika
  • Cumin
  • Chili powder
  • Oregano
  • Optional jalapeño

Steps

  1. Brown turkey with onion and peppers.
  2. Add garlic, 2 tbsp chili powder, 1 tbsp each cumin and smoked paprika, oregano.
  3. Stir in lentils, tomatoes, and 3 cups water; simmer 25–30 min until lentils break down and it thickens.

Notes

  • Storage: freezes beautifully — fridge Mon–Wed, freezer Thu–Fri. Good Tuesday-dinner-scale-up candidate.
