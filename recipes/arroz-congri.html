Arroz Congrí — Mom's Recipe

Servings: See notes

Difficulty: Easy

From: Sept 29, 2026

Mom's recipe, translated from her Spanish original

ricebeanscubanfamily

Mom's arroz congrí, translated from her Spanish original into plain text for an Apple Note.

Ingredients

  • 1 can black beans (15.5 oz) — save the liquid from the can
  • 2 cups long-grain rice
  • 2 tablespoons sofrito
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 2 chicken bouillon cubes
  • 2 cups water + the bean liquid
  • 1/4 cup oil (not olive oil)
  • 1 teaspoon olives and capers (optional)
  • Salt (if needed)

Steps

  1. Open the can of beans, drain the liquid, and save it for later.
  2. In the pot where you'll make the rice, heat the oil over medium-high heat. When hot, add the sofrito and the chicken bouillon cubes, stir, and cook for one minute.
  3. Add the rice and stir so it mixes well with the sofrito. Then add the black beans, the bay leaf, and the olives (optional).
  4. Add the 2 cups of water and the bean liquid, stir, and taste the water to check the seasoning. If it's bland, add a little salt.
  5. Cook until you see the rice is drier, then stir, cover, and turn to low heat until the rice is done (about 20 to 30 minutes).
