Flan

Servings: See notes

Difficulty: Medium

From: Oct 27, 2026 (migrated from Notion)

dessertcustard

Classic baked custard with a dark caramel coating, chilled at least a day before serving. From Angel's own note; fat-free or low-fat variants of the dairy all work.

Ingredients

  • 1 can evaporated milk
  • 1 can condensed milk
  • 4–6 eggs
  • 8 oz cream cheese
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 3 tbsp water

Steps

Caramel

  1. Microwave the sugar and water until light caramel color (~6–8 minutes on high). Let it rest ~15 seconds until it darkens.
  2. Pour the caramel into the mold and swirl quickly to coat the sides and bottom — it hardens fast.

Flan

  1. Blend the flan ingredients and pour into the mold.
  2. Set the mold in a larger container filled with water (water bath).
  3. Bake at 350°F for 1 hour 20 minutes. Test with a toothpick — the center should not be liquid.
  4. Cool to room temperature, then refrigerate at least 24 hours (48 hours is better) before serving.

Notes

  • Servings were not stated in the source note.
  • Chill at least 24 hours; 48 hours is better.
