Herb-Crusted Rack of Lamb with White Bean Puree & Gremolata

Servings: 2

Difficulty: Hard

Per serving: ~65g protein per serving

From: Oct 24, 2026

lambdinner

Herb-crusted rack of lamb with white bean puree and gremolata. 1 hr 30 min total, ~45 min active. The gremolata is the bright, fresh contrast that ties the dish together.

Ingredients

  • 1 frenched rack of lamb (8 bones, ~1.5 lb)
  • 3 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1/2 cup panko breadcrumbs
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced, divided
  • 2 tbsp rosemary, chopped
  • 1 tbsp thyme, chopped
  • 2 tbsp parsley, chopped, plus a large handful for gremolata
  • 3 tbsp parmesan, grated
  • 1 can (15 oz) cannellini beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1/2 cup chicken or veggie stock
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 2 lemons (zest + juice)
  • Olive oil (extra virgin)
  • Kosher salt & black pepper
  • Red chili flakes (optional)

Steps

Part 1 — herb crust & searing (30 min, start first)

  1. Bring lamb to room temp & season: remove the rack from the fridge 45-60 min before cooking. Pat dry with paper towels. Season generously all over with salt and pepper. This is the single most important step for even cooking.
  2. Make the herb crust: combine panko, 2 cloves minced garlic, rosemary, thyme, parsley, parmesan, a pinch of salt, and 2 tbsp olive oil. Mix until it holds together loosely like wet sand. Set aside.
  3. Sear the lamb: heat a cast iron or oven-safe skillet over high heat until smoking. Add 1 tbsp olive oil. Sear the lamb fat-side down for 3-4 minutes until deep golden brown. Flip and sear the other sides briefly, 1 min each. Remove from heat. Let cool 5 minutes.
  4. Apply mustard and crust: brush the fat side and meat side generously with Dijon mustard. Press the herb crust firmly onto the mustard-coated surface in an even layer. The mustard is the glue — be generous.
  5. Roast: preheat oven to 400F. Place the lamb crust-side up in the skillet (or a roasting pan). Roast for 18-22 minutes for medium-rare (internal temp 130-135F). Tent loosely with foil and rest for 10 minutes before cutting — do not skip this.

Part 2 — white bean puree (can run parallel, ~20 min)

  1. Saute garlic: in a saucepan, warm 1 tbsp olive oil over medium heat. Add 1 clove minced garlic and cook until fragrant, about 1 minute. Don't let it brown.
  2. Simmer beans: add the drained, rinsed cannellini beans to the pan with the chicken stock. Simmer on medium-low for 10 minutes until the liquid reduces slightly and beans are very soft.
  3. Blend & finish puree: transfer to a blender (or use an immersion blender). Add 1 tbsp butter, a squeeze of lemon juice, salt, and pepper. Blitz until completely smooth and silky. Taste and adjust seasoning. Keep warm on low heat.

Part 3 — gremolata (5 min, make anytime)

  1. Combine: mix together zest of 1 lemon, a large handful of finely chopped parsley, 1 small clove garlic (minced or grated), a pinch of chili flakes (optional), and 1 tbsp olive oil. Season with salt. This should be bright, fresh, and punchy — it's the contrast element that ties the whole dish together.

Plating

  1. Spoon a generous swoosh of white bean puree onto each plate. Slice the rack between the bones into individual chops (2-3 per person for a main course). Rest the chops upright, leaning against each other. Spoon gremolata over the top. Finish with a drizzle of good olive oil and a wedge of lemon on the side.

Notes

  • Pro tip: wrap the exposed rib bones in foil before roasting so they don't char. Remove the foil for the last 5 minutes if you want a bit of color on them.
