Turkey Chili Verde with White Beans

Servings: ~5 lunch portions

Per serving: ~480 kcal, 42g protein, 11g fiber per serving

From: Oct 4, 2026

meal-prepturkeyone-potfreezer-friendly

One-pot, freezer-friendly, highest fiber of its round. Suggested in the Oct 4 round; set aside that week.

Ingredients

  • 1.25 lb ground turkey
  • 2 cans white beans (cannellini), drained
  • 24 oz jar salsa verde
  • 1 cup frozen corn
  • 1 yellow onion, diced
  • 1 jalapeño, diced
  • 2 cups low-sodium chicken broth
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 2 limes
  • Fresh cilantro
  • Salt to taste
  • Plain Greek yogurt (dollop at lunch, instead of sour cream)

Steps

  1. Brown ground turkey in a large pot over medium-high, breaking it up — 6–8 min. No extra oil needed; drain if much fat renders.
  2. Add diced onion and jalapeño; cook 5 min. Stir in minced garlic, 2 tsp cumin, 1 tsp oregano; 1 min more.
  3. Pour in salsa verde and broth; bring to a boil, then simmer 20 min.
  4. Stir in drained beans and corn; simmer 10 more min until slightly thickened. Salt to taste.
  5. Pack with lime wedges; dollop Greek yogurt instead of sour cream at lunch.
