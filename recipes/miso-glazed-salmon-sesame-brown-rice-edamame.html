Miso-Glazed Salmon with Sesame Brown Rice & Edamame

Servings: ~5 lunch portions

Per serving: ~650 kcal, 45g protein, 9g fiber per serving

From: Oct 4, 2026

meal-prepsalmon

Rich-tasting without heavy oil; the glaze caramelizes under the broiler. Suggested in the Oct 4 round; set aside that week.

Ingredients

  • 5 × 5 oz salmon fillets
  • 3 tbsp white miso paste
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
  • 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
  • 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
  • 2 tbsp water (glaze)
  • 1.5 cups brown rice
  • 2.5 cups frozen shelled edamame
  • 2 heads broccoli
  • Sesame seeds
  • Light spray of oil and salt (broccoli)

Steps

  1. Cook 1.5 cups brown rice per package directions (do this first — it takes the longest).
  2. Whisk glaze: 3 tbsp white miso, 1 tbsp honey, 1 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tsp grated ginger, 1 tsp sesame oil, 2 tbsp water.
  3. Set oven to broil. Lay salmon on a foil-lined pan, brush generously with glaze, broil 6–8 min until the edges char slightly.
  4. Microwave or steam edamame; roast broccoli florets at 425°F for 15 min with a light spray of oil and salt — pull them while still bright green (no mush).
  5. Pack: brown rice, salmon, edamame, broccoli; sprinkle sesame seeds on top.
