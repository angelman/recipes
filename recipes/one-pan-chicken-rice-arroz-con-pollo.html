One-Pan Chicken & Rice (Arroz con Pollo)

Servings: Roughly 5–6

Difficulty: Easy

Per serving: ~600 cal, ~40g protein per serving (estimate)

From: Sept 27, 2026

From @moniquerubalcava

chickenone-panrice

One-pan chicken and rice, extracted from the creator's post. Tagged #mealprep by the creator.

Ingredients

  • 2–3 lbs chicken thighs
  • 2 cups rice
  • 4 cups chicken broth or water
  • 1 red bell pepper, diced
  • ½ medium onion, diced
  • ½ cup carrots, diced
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2–3 tbsp butter
  • Olive oil
  • 1–2 tbsp chicken bouillon
  • Salt, to taste
  • Fresh cilantro, for garnish
  • Paprika, onion powder, garlic powder, Goya Adobo seasoning

Steps

  1. Dice the bell pepper, onion, and carrots; mince the garlic.
  2. Season both sides of the chicken thighs with paprika, onion powder, garlic powder, and Adobo.
  3. Heat olive oil in a large pan over medium heat. Sear chicken ~4 minutes per side until golden. Remove and set aside.
  4. In the same pan, add the vegetables and garlic with the butter. Sauté a few minutes until fragrant.
  5. Add the rice and toast with the vegetables 1–2 minutes.
  6. Pour in the broth and stir in the bouillon. Taste and adjust salt/bouillon.
  7. Nestle the chicken back on top of the rice, pouring in any collected juices.
  8. Cover and cook on medium-low ~20 minutes, until rice is tender and chicken hits 165°F.
  9. Rest covered off the heat 5–10 minutes.
  10. Garnish with cilantro and serve.
