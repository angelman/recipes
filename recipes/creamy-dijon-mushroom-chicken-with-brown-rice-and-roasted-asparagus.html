Creamy Dijon Mushroom Chicken with Brown Rice & Roasted Asparagus

Servings: 4

Difficulty: Medium

From: Oct 27, 2026 (migrated from Notion)

chickendinner

Seared chicken cutlets in a mushroom Dijon cream sauce, served over brown rice with roasted asparagus. The sauce is especially good spooned directly over the rice.

Ingredients

  • 1½ cups dry brown rice
  • Water per package or rice-cooker instructions
  • 1½ lb boneless, skinless chicken breasts
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 12–16 oz mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 cup chicken broth
  • ½ cup heavy cream
  • 1½ tbsp Dijon mustard
  • ½ tsp dried thyme
  • ¼ cup fresh parsley, chopped
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 1 tsp lemon juice, optional
  • 1 lb asparagus
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt and black pepper
  • ½ lemon

Steps

  1. Start the brown rice first (it takes longest); cook per package and keep warm.
  2. Roast the asparagus: oven 425°F, trim woody ends, toss with olive oil, salt, and pepper; roast 12–16 minutes in a single layer; finish with lemon.
  3. Slice thick chicken breasts horizontally into ½–¾ inch cutlets; season with salt and pepper; sear in olive oil over medium-high heat, 3–4 minutes per side, until browned (not fully cooked yet); set aside.
  4. In the same skillet over medium heat, melt the butter and brown the mushrooms undisturbed in batches so they brown rather than steam, 6–8 minutes; add garlic and thyme, cook 30–60 seconds.
  5. Add chicken broth, scrape up browned bits, simmer 2–3 minutes; reduce to medium-low, add cream and Dijon, simmer gently 3–5 minutes until slightly thickened; season to taste.
  6. Return chicken and juices to the skillet, spoon sauce over, simmer gently 2–4 minutes until the thickest part reaches 165°F; stir in parsley and lemon juice if using.
  7. Serve over brown rice with the sauce spooned over, asparagus alongside.
