Mediterranean Tuna & Chickpea Power Bowls

Servings: ~5 portions

Per serving: ~580 kcal, 46g protein, 10g fiber per serving

From: Oct 4, 2026

meal-preptunano-cookchickpea

No-cook protein, lemony and briny. Hearty without being heavy.

Ingredients

  • 4 cans (5 oz) albacore tuna, drained
  • 2 cans (15 oz) chickpeas, drained
  • 1.5 cups dry pearled farro
  • 1 English cucumber, diced
  • 1 pint cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1 cup kalamata olives
  • 4 oz feta cheese, crumbled
  • 2 lemons, juiced
  • 1 small red onion, thin-sliced
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt, pepper, pinch of dried oregano

Steps

  1. Simmer 1.5 cups farro in salted water ~25 min until chewy; drain.
  2. Drain tuna and chickpeas into a big bowl. Add diced cucumber, halved tomatoes, thin-sliced red onion, olives.
  3. Whisk dressing: juice of 2 lemons, 3 tbsp olive oil, salt, pepper, pinch of dried oregano.
  4. Toss everything with the dressing and crumbled feta. Pack over the farro.
