Israeli Hummus Recipe

Servings: See notes

Difficulty: Easy

From: Oct 27, 2026 (migrated from Notion)

Source: cookieandkate.com

dipsnack

Ultra-smooth hummus with Angel's modifications: half the lemon juice of the base recipe, chickpeas chilled before blending, and no cumin.

Ingredients

  • 1 can (15 oz) chickpeas, rinsed and drained
  • ½ tsp baking soda
  • ⅛ cup lemon juice
  • 3 small cloves garlic, roughly chopped
  • ½ tsp fine sea salt, to taste
  • ½ cup tahini
  • 5 tbsp ice-cold water
  • 1 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil

Steps

  1. Boil chickpeas with baking soda, covered by several inches of water, ~20 minutes; drain, rinse 30 seconds, then chill in fridge or freezer.
  2. Process lemon juice, garlic, and salt until the garlic is very finely chopped; rest 10+ minutes so the garlic mellows.
  3. Add tahini; blend until thick and creamy, scraping down the sides.
  4. With the processor running, drizzle in 4 tbsp ice water; blend until ultra smooth, pale, and creamy.
  5. Add the chilled chickpeas; blend while drizzling in the olive oil, ~2 minutes, scraping down as needed, adding more ice water by the tablespoon until super creamy.

Notes

  • Angel's modifications: he used ⅛ cup lemon juice (half the base recipe) and would try 3/16 cup next time; 5 tablespoons ice water; chickpeas chilled in fridge or freezer before blending; no cumin.
  • The 'try 3/16 cup lemon next time' note is an open experiment, not a settled quantity.
