Chicken Shawarma Bowls

Servings: 5 portions

Difficulty: Easy

Per serving: ~560 cal, ~46g protein, ~11g fiber per serving

From: Oct 25, 2026

meal-prepchickenhigh-proteinhigh-fiber

Whole thighs seared then sliced, with brown rice, chickpeas and chopped veg, topped with a yogurt-lemon sauce. The Oct 25 meal-prep pick.

Ingredients

  • 2 lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs
  • Shawarma Spice (already at home), salt, pepper
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 cups brown rice, dry
  • 2 cans chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 2 cucumbers
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 red onion
  • Plain Greek yogurt (1 container)
  • Lemon juice (bottled; no fresh lemon needed)
  • Jarred garlic, at home

Steps

  1. Toss the chicken in Shawarma Spice, salt, and 1 tbsp oil. Rest 20 minutes.
  2. Sear the thighs whole in a hot skillet, 5–6 minutes per side, until 165°F. Rest, then slice.
  3. While the chicken rests, cook the brown rice and drain and rinse the chickpeas.
  4. Make the sauce: Greek yogurt mixed with lemon juice, a spoon of jarred garlic, and salt.
  5. Assemble bowls: rice, sliced chicken, chickpeas, cucumber, tomato, and red onion. Top with the yogurt-lemon sauce.
  6. Portion into 5 containers for the week's work lunches.

Notes

  • Sear the thighs whole, rest, then slice — slicing after cooking keeps them juicier than cutting them up raw.
  • Chickpeas need no cooking or seasoning here: just drain and rinse.
  • This batch makes 5 portions; for a Tuesday dinner scale-up, add an extra portion's worth of chicken and rice.
